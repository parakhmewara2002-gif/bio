Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Biodata & Resume Studio ("the website", "we", "our") is a free, static web application hosted on GitHub Pages. This page explains — accurately, and matching how the website is actually built — how your information is handled.

In short: this website does not have a server-side database. The forms you fill in (name, date of birth, family details, resume information, your photo, etc.) are processed directly in your own browser to build your document. That data is not intentionally transmitted to any developer-controlled server.

What information you enter

When you use the Biodata Builder or Resume Builder, you may enter information such as your name, date of birth, address, phone number, email, education, occupation, family details, a photograph, and other resume or biodata content.

How this information is processed

  • The website runs entirely as client-side code in your browser.
  • Your document (biodata or resume) is generated and rendered directly in your browser.
  • The PDF you download is created locally in your browser — it is not generated on, or uploaded to, a server first.
  • We do not operate a backend database that stores the biodata or resume content you type in.

Local browser storage (drafts)

To help you avoid losing progress, the website may save a draft of your in-progress form using your browser's own LocalStorage. This data is stored only on your own device, inside your browser — it is not stored on our server, and we cannot access it.

  • You can clear this saved draft at any time using the Clear option in the app, or by clearing your browser's site data/storage.
  • This local storage is not guaranteed to be permanent — clearing your browser data, using a different browser, or using a different device will not carry the draft over.

Photographs

A photo you upload for your biodata is used only to render your document in your browser and is not intentionally uploaded to a developer-controlled server.

No account, no login

You do not need to create an account, log in, or verify your phone/email to use this website.

No payments

This website is completely free. We do not collect payment information because there is no paid feature.

Advertising and tracking

We do not add analytics, advertising trackers, or user-profiling scripts to this website by default.

Third-party services

The website loads some public resources (such as web fonts and open-source libraries used for PDF generation) from third-party content delivery networks so the page can render and function. These requests are standard for loading a webpage's resources and are not used to transmit your biodata/resume content.

Selling data

We do not sell your information because we do not collect or store it on a server in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If the website's functionality changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated to accurately reflect the actual implementation.
